The chisel that
once carved for
the Emperor. 皇 室 に 献 上 さ れ た 技

"My grandfather's hands shaped a tribute for the Crown Prince — now Emperor of Japan. Three generations later, those same techniques live in mine."

Founded in 1935 by master carver Ijiri Suiun, our workshop has shaped the woodcarving tradition of Shiga's Kaminyu village for ninety years. The first generation was renowned enough to be entrusted with creating presentation pieces for the Imperial Household — a recognition reserved for only the finest artisans of the era.

He was also the chief carver for some of Kansai's most prestigious danjiri (festival floats), including those of Gokurakuji-cho in Kishiwada. His name became synonymous with sculptural depth, lifelike movement, and uncompromising craftsmanship.

Today, that lineage continues through Kazushige Ijiri, the third-generation master — certified Dento-Kogei-shi (Traditional Craftsman) of Hikone Butsudan, one of Japan's most prestigious nationally-designated craft traditions.

The art of Suiun-bori

Three signatures of
the Suiun lineage. 翠 雲 彫 の 三 つ の 特 徴

Suiun-bori is not simply a technique — it is a way of seeing wood. Where many carving schools flatten and stylize, our lineage insists on volume, motion, and the breath of life within each form. Three principles define our work.

01

Sculptural Depth 写 実 的 立 体 感

Suiun-bori is celebrated for its shajitsu-teki rittai-kan — a quality of three-dimensional realism rarely matched in flat-relief traditions. Our works breathe.

02

Sacred Range 幅 広 い 制 作 領 域

From temple guardians and Buddhist altars to danjiri festival floats, fine ranma screens, and intimate household pieces — one master's hand traverses every scale.

03

Tradition into Today 現 代 へ の 革 新

The third generation extends the craft into modern altars, pet memorials, signage and accessories — proving heritage need not be frozen to remain authentic.

How a piece

Every commission begins with a conversation — and ends with a piece that exists only for you. We work with clients across Japan, North America, Europe, and Asia.

Share your vision via our form, video call, or email. We respond within 48 hours in English or Japanese.
